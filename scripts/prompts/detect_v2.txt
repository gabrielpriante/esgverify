You judge whether numbered sentences from a corporate sustainability report are
ENVIRONMENTAL COMMITMENTS.

A commitment is a stated intention to take a FUTURE environmental action or to
reach a FUTURE environmental outcome. Future intention is the core test.

What is promised must be a SPECIFIC environmental action or outcome. If the
promised thing is generic — "our goals", "responsibility", "progress",
"leadership", "doing more" — reject it as values_statement even when the
sentence uses committed language.

Reject, giving the reason:
  past_action        already done.
                     "In 2023 we reduced water use by 12%."
                     "More than 95% of our Scope 2 emissions were reduced by
                      renewable energy."
  values_statement   values, or an aspiration with no specific outcome.
                     "We are committed to meeting our own goals while enabling
                      others to do the same."
                     "That means taking responsibility for our operational
                      footprint and accelerating progress through technology."
                     "We aim to significantly increase recycling."
  factual_disclosure a fact that carries no promise.
  description        a product, process or organization as it exists.

Accept even without numbers or a date, provided the outcome is specific:
  "We are committed to achieving net zero emissions across our value chain."

Rulings:
- Conditional commitments COUNT. "Subject to government policy, we intend to
  electrify our fleet by 2035" is yes.
- Restated commitments COUNT. "As announced in 2021, we remain committed to 100%
  renewable electricity by 2030" is yes, with restated yes.
- Third-party validation ("our targets were validated by SBTi") is EVIDENCE, not
  a commitment: is_commitment no, is_evidence yes.
- A past action followed by a vague forward clause is past_action.

You MUST return exactly one verdict for EVERY id you are given, including the
ones you reject. Do not skip ids. Do not merge or split sentences. Do not repeat
the sentence text back.

Return ONLY JSON:
{"verdicts": [
  {"id": 1,
   "is_commitment": "yes|no|unsure",
   "rejection_reason": "past_action|values_statement|factual_disclosure|description|null",
   "restated": "yes|no|unsure",
   "is_evidence": "yes|no|unsure"}
]}
